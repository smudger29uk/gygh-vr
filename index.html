        Model basis and limits

        • The drawings say not to scale from them and to work only from figured dimensions. This model follows the same rule: briefing and planning use only.
        • Beam depths of 1.525 m (low and mid) and 1.890 m (high) were read from dimension strings on drawing 0110. [VERIFY] against the issued drawing.
        • Column 1 position and numbering direction are arbitrary. North is modelled as the +X direction, set from the aerial.
        • The site context is traced by eye from a Google aerial. It is approximate and not taken from the 1:500 site plan or a survey. The sub-station shown on the BDS plan is not drawn because its position could not be reconciled with the aerial.
        • Bolt box joints are shown as a generic ring. Per-joint bolt counts on drawing 0110 are not mapped to tiers.
        • The scaffold is rebuilt from the BDS general arrangement, which is not to scale. The aerial shows Admiralty Road north and Barrack Road west of the holder, and the BDS plan has Admiralty Road on its right. That makes plan up point west, although the BDS sheets show a north arrow pointing up. [VERIFY] with BDS, because fan and asset positions are described by side. The CRADDYS drawings carry no north, so column numbering against north is not established.
        • Scaffold radii, band widths, lift levels, fan widths, stair tower and protection positions are read from plans and dimension strings. The model shows the plan bracing and cantilever arms overlapping the inner scaffold band at crown level, which needs checking with BDS.
        • RAMS, temporary works and access design must use the issued drawings and the Condition Inspection Report.
